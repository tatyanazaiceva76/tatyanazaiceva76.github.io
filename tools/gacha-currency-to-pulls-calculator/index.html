What this calculator does

This converter uses only the exchange rate you enter. It does not guess which game you play, assume a premium-currency bundle, or attach a cash value to the result. That makes it useful for any banner system where one pull consumes a fixed number of currency units.

Only complete pulls count. The calculator divides the balance by the cost per pull, rounds down to a whole pull, and keeps the remainder as leftover currency. Saved tickets are added after conversion because they do not reduce the entered balance.

Use the current in-game cost for one independently available pull. Discounted or indivisible multi-pull bundles are not modeled: an average per-pull bundle price can overstate what your balance can buy.

Formula

whole pulls = floor(currency ÷ cost per pull); total = whole pulls + saved tickets

Example: With 12,800 currency, a cost of 160 per pull, and 10 tickets, the converter returns 80 currency-funded pulls, 90 total pulls, and zero currency left over.

FAQ

Why does the calculator round down?

A fractional result cannot normally be used as a pull, so the tool counts only complete conversions and reports the remainder separately.

Does this include paid currency packs?

No. It performs an in-game unit conversion only and makes no price or purchase recommendation.

Can I use tickets and currency together?

Yes. Enter tickets under saved pulls; they are added after the currency conversion.
