FAQ

What is pity in gacha games?

Pity is a mercy counter: the more you pull without a top-rarity drop, the higher your odds become, up to a hard cap where the next pull is guaranteed. Genshin, Star Rail and ZZZ cap at 90; Wuthering Waves caps at 80.

What is soft pity?

Soft pity is the pull range (roughly the mid-70s for character banners) where the per-pull 5★ rate jumps sharply above its ~0.6% base. Most 5★ drops actually happen inside the soft-pity window, well before the hard cap.

What is the 50/50?

On a limited character banner, a 5★ has a 50% chance to be the featured unit. If you lose (get a standard 5★), the next 5★ is guaranteed to be the featured one — so worst case is two hard pities.

Does this calculator use real drop rates?

It uses the published hard-pity, soft-pity-start and featured-rate rules plus the standard community-datamined soft-pity curve. Results are exact probabilities under that model — accurate estimates, not official guarantees.
