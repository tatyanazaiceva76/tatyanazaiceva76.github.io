Methodology and sources

PityLab computes a probability distribution with dynamic programming. It does not simulate random pulls, predict your account, connect to a game client, or guarantee an outcome.

What the engine calculates

For each pull, the engine advances the selected banner's pity state, applies the disclosed top-rarity rate, then applies the featured-target rule. A non-featured top-rarity result resets pity and advances the guarantee state. Multiple-copy results convolve the one-copy distributions. The number shown is exact only for this documented model.

Known limits

  • Publishers can change rates, guarantees, banner pools and carry-over rules. Check the in-game Details screen before spending currency.
  • Community soft-pity approximations are not publisher-certified per-pull tables.
  • Bonus systems, special tiles, rebates, free pulls, currency prices and account-specific history beyond the entered pity/guarantee state are excluded.
  • The Azur Promilia beta sandbox is preserved at its old URL but is noindex and absent from navigation/sitemap because no current official rate table was verified.

Update and correction policy

Rules were reviewed on 22 July 2026. Publisher or in-game rules override this site. If a rule cannot be verified, the affected page is excluded from search rather than silently presented as current.
